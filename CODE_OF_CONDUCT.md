# Code of Conduct

Runcraft is a friendly project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else, is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give constructive feedback about code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and explain your reasoning when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission
- Trolling, spam or repeated off-topic comments

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or block participants who do not follow this code. To report a problem privately, email thekirankumarv@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential.
